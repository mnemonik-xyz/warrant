The warrant

Every decision becomes a signed, reproducible record.

warrant {
  policy_hash:  0x7f3a…c21e
  facts_hash:   0x91bd…04aa
  evaluator_id: verus-eval-v1
  decision:     Allow
  signature:    COSE_Sign1
}
Re-run the check, get the same answer.
Counterparties and auditors verify without trusting either agent.
Built on Mnemonik.
Signed, encrypted negotiation; verifiable identities; warrants stored permanently on decentralized storage.
