# Warrant: one-minute presentation script

Six scenes. Each paragraph is the voiceover for one slide.

## 1. Title
Warrant. Trust between AI agents, without trusting the agent.

## 2. Problem
AI agents are starting to move money: payments, invoices, cross-chain swaps. But a model can make mistakes, misread a request, or be manipulated by a malicious prompt. Saying "be careful" in a prompt is not a control.

## 3. Policy
Warrant does not trust the agent. The owner writes their intent as a policy in a small, fixed language: no more than this amount per deal, only these counterparties. The agent reads and proposes. It never decides, and it cannot pay.

## 4. Evaluator
A formally verified evaluator applies the policy to checked facts and returns Allow, Ask, or Deny. A machine-checked proof shows the decision follows the policy and nothing else. The key that moves money is held by a separate signer, never by the agent.

## 5. Warrant record
Every decision becomes a signed warrant: the policy, the facts, the result. Anyone can re-run the check and get the same answer. Built on Mnemonik, with signed identities and permanent storage, so what was agreed can be proven later.

## 6. Close
Autonomy bounded by proofs, not prompts. That is Warrant.
